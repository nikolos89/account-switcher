Sign-in data is encrypted for your Windows user. Email and labels are stored as local metadata.

Add another account
  1. Save the current account using the button above.
  2. Open Settings → your account → Sign out, then sign in to another account through Google.
  3. Return to this panel and click Save current account.

Do this once per account. After that, select a saved profile in this panel.

Switching restarts the main Antigravity App. Add Profile Sidebar for a shortcut from your profile menu.

Companion: Profile Sidebar shows your account and plan below Settings.
